## 1. What you must decide

Nothing below was decided for you when a client reads the result. For each point I say what I recommend.

1. **The « current bill » printed on the quote.** A client who says « 900 MAD per month » reads « ≈ 831 MAD per
   month » on the PDF. Cause: with the stepped tariff, no consumption gives exactly 900 MAD (510 kWh gives 831 MAD, one
   kWh more gives 954 MAD), and the engine keeps the lower side on purpose. Three ways to say it: (a) keep the numbers
   and add one sentence that explains it; (b) print the 900 MAD the client said and compute the savings on it (less
   careful); (c) call the 831 « bill recomputed with the tariff ». I recommend (a). Nothing was changed.
2. **Switching on « rules level 15 ».** Several fixes that change what a quote PDF prints are ready but switched off,
   because a quote already sent must stay as the client received it: one rounding (7 987 / 7 988 MAD on two pages of the
   same PDF), one false sentence on the industrial PDF (« your declared hours » when the hours were assumed), one
   sentence that announces a section that is not printed. They come on together with the production taken from the real
   roof layout, the day you approve that level.
3. **A lead with consumption in kWh and no bill** can now get an automatic quote (the engine can compute it). Before,
   the screen asked for a bill. I recommend keeping this. Your rule « typed kWh that contradict the bills block the
   quote » is untouched.
4. **Origin of the water measurements on an agricultural lead.** The card now lets the seller choose « given by the
   driller » or « pumping test », and refuses « measured during the visit » (only a visit can set it). Before, the
   choice was silently replaced by « declared by the client ». This goes against the older rule that these origins are
   never typed by hand. Keep, or go back?
5. **Name shown at the top of the workshop.** It is now the calepinage's name from start to end (the mockup shows it;
   the documents the client receives already print it). An earlier task had put the lead's name there. Keep?
6. **A sent quote whose lead's bill changes.** One existing function rewrites the sizing block of a SENT quote when the
   bill on the lead changes. Two of your decisions disagree here (a sent quote can be corrected on site; a sent quote is
   never changed). I measured it and changed nothing. Which rule wins?
7. **The Technicien role** gets « no permission » on the calepinage, on the phone survey and on the CRM in the copy of
   production. If technicians are meant to do the roof survey on their phone, the role needs these rights.
8. **Pumping quotes cannot be made automatically today, in production.** The product itself lists four missing things:
   the price of the 11 pumps that have a maker's curve, the price of the ground structure, the installation price (fixed
   part and per panel), and no single-phase pump has a maker's curve. For the test I put public prices on the copy (see
   section 5); I could not source your installation price, so I left it empty.
9. **The map key** for the test copy: I do not type a production key myself. Without it the test runs on a grey map
   (drawing works; address search does not).
10. **Client name on the PDF.** The full document changes « U3-i Ferme forage » into « U3-I Ferme Forage »; the
    one-page document prints it as typed. Which one do you want?
11. **Lead that wants a battery.** The automatic quote then carries only the « with battery » option, with nothing to
    compare. Intended, or both options?
12. **« 3 pages ».** The house note and the PDF dialog say the residential quote has 3 pages. With two options, a
    discount and a rounding line it has 4, on purpose (the « Rentabilité » block moves to its own page so the footer
    does not cut it). Correct the note, or the layout?
13. **Summer consumption in kWh.** The lead has no field for it (only a summer bill in MAD). Add one?
14. **Manual quote for an agricultural site whose electrical phase is unknown.** The automatic quote is blocked, but
    the full editor can still create the quote and send it. Block the sending, mark the PDF « preliminary », or leave?
15. **« Envoyer le lien » in the client view** is the only gold button and it is disabled (it points to the lead card).
    In front of a client the announced action does not work. Enable it there, or remove it from that screen?

## 2. What I chose without you (inside the product, can be undone)

- A simulation never runs on the centre of the map. If the site pin was never placed, it uses the reference city of

  runs one decision again to build the lines and copies the stored study blocks. Same engine, same inputs, checked
  against the frozen examples.

- When a lead is erased (your legal erasure), its stored designs are deleted and nothing is recomputed afterwards.
- The label « Production ÷ consommation » stays as it is; the mockup wrote « 82 % of your bill », which is not what the
  number is. The wording is yours to choose.
- The 14-screen mockup comparison test runs on demand, not every night (it would be red every night while screens are
  still being brought to the mockups).
- Two shared development containers were stopped to free memory; they are restarted at the end.
